The problem

Buying and selling should not depend on word of mouth.

Most campus and local trade still happens in scattered WhatsApp groups, statuses and DMs. Useful items stay hidden, and serious buyers never see them.

01

Students get stuck with unused items

Textbooks, gadgets and hostel belongings sit idle because buyers are hard to find.

02

Buyers cannot see what is available

They rely on groups and referrals instead of one searchable catalogue.

03

Small stores have low visibility

Local retailers need a simple storefront that puts their products in front of buyers.

How it works

From listing to enquiry in a few steps.

  1. 1

    Create a seller profile

    Students and store owners sign up and describe what they sell.

  2. 2

    Upload a product

    Add images, description, price, condition and category.

  3. 3

    Get discovered

    Buyers browse, search and filter to find the item they need.

  4. 4

    Contact the seller

    Buyers ask questions, then both sides agree on payment and collection.

MVP focus

Listings first. Everything else later.

Authentication, seller profiles, product discovery, search, contact and admin controls come before payments, delivery tracking or reviews.
