Our vision

A trusted marketplace for campus and local trade.

AutoStore gives sellers a storefront and buyers one place to discover what is available nearby.

Back to home → How it works

MVP scope

What we are building first.

Six focused building blocks ship before we add anything complicated.

01

User authentication

Register and log in as a buyer or seller.

02

Seller profiles

Students and store owners manage their public profile.

03

Product listings

Upload images, descriptions, prices and categories.

04

Discovery and search

Browse, search and filter by product name, category and price.

05

Seller contact

Buyers enquire before agreeing on payment and collection.

06

Admin controls

Manage users, products and reported listings.

Later phases

Payments, delivery and reviews come after validation.

We are starting with simple listings and real conversations between buyers and sellers.
